Midnight at the Sanatorium

Managed to gain access to the main medical wing just before midnight. The peeling paint on the ceiling looks like cracked earth under a flashlight. Dust levels were incredibly high; glad I brought a proper respirator mask. Spent three hours capturing long exposures of the main operating theater.

The Forgotten Rail Yard

An easier exploration during golden hour. Multiple vintage train cars left sitting on overgrown tracks. Nature is completely reclaiming the steel chassis. Discovered a locker room with old worker logs dating back to the late 1970s.
